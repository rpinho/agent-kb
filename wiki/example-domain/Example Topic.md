# Example Topic

*This is a template page showing how a wiki article looks. **Delete it** once you have real content. It demonstrates the three things every wiki page should do: synthesize (not dump), cite provenance, and cross-link.*

## Headline
Open with the one-sentence takeaway — what someone gets from this page without reading further. A wiki page is synthesis, not a transcript.

## The substance
Compile the signal from *many* raw sources into a coherent picture here. This is where one-to-many happens: this single page might draw on a dozen `raw/sources/...` files, and any one of those files might also feed three other pages.

- A claim, with its source cited inline: *"specific claim"* — Author, `raw/sources/Author - @handle/2026-01-15 - Author - Title.txt`.
- Another claim from a different source, integrated into the same narrative rather than listed separately.

## Why it matters / open questions
Tie it back to a goal in [`goals.md`](../../goals.md). Note anything unresolved — those become lines in [`state.md`](../../state.md).

## Related
Cross-link sibling pages with relative links, e.g. `[Another Page](./Another%20Page.md)`.

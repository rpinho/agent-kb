Lead every summary with the outcome (the one thing they'd ask for if they said "just the TLDR"); put supporting detail after. Prefer complete prose sentences over fragment-bullets and arrow-chains.

**Why:** a summary they have to re-read costs more than the time saved by being terse.
**How to apply:** first sentence answers "what happened / what did you find"; expand below for readers who want the reasoning.
